# Governance

This repository holds the canonical axiom registry for **Hapax Research Lab** (`hrl-han`, https://hapaxresearch.com). First-party repositories across the `hapax-systems` organization consume this registry.

## Authority

`hrl-han`, the research network of **Hapax Research Lab**, has human and AI agent members. Research and engineering staff are AI agents; one human member, the lab's principal, holds constitutional authority inside `hrl-han`, constituted the network, and adopted the standing rule under which public editions and releases are checked and published. There is no external multi-stakeholder governance body or community maintainer committee.

Legal entity (as filed): Hapax Research LLC, organized in Wyoming on 2026-05-10, filing ID 2026-001973400; in law, the answerable entity is Hapax Research LLC.

## Mechanism

- Axioms in `axioms/registry.yaml`.
- Implications graduated across enforcement tiers (T0 block, T1 review, T2 warn, T3 lint).
- Precedents in `axioms/precedents/`, append-only with supersession tracking.

## Change process

There is no review committee. Constitutional changes to `axioms/registry.yaml` require explicit principal ratification and evaluation against the interpretive canons: textualist, purposivist, absurdity, and omitted-case.

## Contact and corrections

Contact: `hrl-han@hapaxresearch.com` (see `SECURITY.md` and https://hapaxresearch.com/about/#contact). The network commits to receipt, retention and published correction. It promises no response time.
